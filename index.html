How tracking works

The camera detects full-body people and assigns a temporary track number while they stay in view. Each new track is counted and logged in SQLite.

It does not use faces, gait signatures, clothing, names, or biometric profiles. When a track is lost or the session ends, it cannot identify that person again.

◆ A cropped JPEG is saved locally when a new track is confirmed. No names, face matching, gait profiles, or cross-session identity are used.

i

Tracking limitation Temporary tracks can split or merge when people cross, leave the frame, or are occluded. This counts detections, not unique individuals across separate visits or sessions.
